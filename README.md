Pharmacy Sales \& Drug Analysis



Project Overview:

This project analyzes pharmacy sales data to identify medicine usage trends and revenue patterns. The analysis evaluates top-selling medicines, monthly sales growth, and provides insights into medicine performance over time.



Dataset Information

\- File: salesdaily.csv

\- Data Period: January 2014 - December 2017

\- Records: Daily sales data for 8 different medicines

\- Medicines Analyzed: M01AB, M01AE, N02BA, N02BE, N05B, N05C, R03, R06

Dataset Columns

\- `datum` - Date of the sales record

\- `M01AB, M01AE, N02BA, N02BE, N05B, N05C, R03, R06` - Sales revenue for each medicine

\- `Year` - Year of the record

\- `Month` - Month of the record

\- `Hour` - Hour/time data

\- `Weekday Name` - Day of the week



&#x20;Analysis Performed:



&#x20;1. Top-Selling Medicines

Identifies which medicines generate the highest revenue and their market share.



&#x20;2. Monthly Sales Growth

Tracks how pharmacy sales change month by month, revealing seasonal patterns and growth trends.



&#x20;3. Sales by Day of Week

Analyzes which days of the week have the highest sales, useful for inventory planning.



&#x20;4. Medicine Sales Distribution

Shows the percentage breakdown of sales across all medicines using a pie chart.



&#x20;5. Individual Medicine Trends

Tracks how each medicine's sales change over the 4-year period.



&#x20;6. Yearly Sales Comparison

Compares total sales revenue across different years to identify growth patterns.
